About Department of Information Technology & Management

Introduction

The Department of Information Technology and Management (ITM) at Ravenshaw University, Cuttack, is a premier center of excellence dedicated to nurturing skilled professionals in the rapidly evolving domains of Information Technology and Business Management.

Established with a vision to bridge the gap between technology and management, the department offers industry-aligned programs that combine technical expertise with managerial acumen.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a leading institution in Eastern India, producing innovative leaders and technocrats who drive digital transformation and sustainable business growth.

Mission: To provide high-quality education, foster research and innovation, and develop ethical professionals capable of addressing real-world challenges in IT and Management.

Academic Programs

  • B.Sc. in Information Technology & Management (ITM) – 3 Years (6 Semesters)
  • M.Sc. / MBA in Information Technology & Management (Proposed)
  • Certificate & Diploma courses in Data Science, Cyber Security, Digital Marketing, and Business Analytics.

Key Highlights

  • Industry-oriented curriculum with focus on AI, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, and Business Intelligence.
  • State-of-the-art computer laboratories and smart classrooms.
  • Strong industry collaboration with companies like TCS, Infosys, Wipro, and Deloitte.
  • Excellent placement record with students placed in top IT & Management firms.
  • Regular workshops, hackathons, and guest lectures by industry experts.
  • Focus on research, innovation, and entrepreneurship development.

Facilities

The department is equipped with modern infrastructure including advanced computing labs, high-speed internet, digital library access, and dedicated incubation center for student startups.

Why Choose ITM Ravenshaw?

Our holistic approach ensures that graduates are not only technically proficient but also possess strong leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills — making them highly sought after by employers across the globe.
